Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Meta Social Manager (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our Instagram analytics and Boost Post proof-of-concept powered by Meta (Facebook) Graph and Marketing APIs.

1. Who we are

Meta Social Manager is a developer / POC application associated with the Meta app break.the.scroll. It helps users connect an Instagram Business or Creator account (via Meta access tokens), view profile and media analytics, and optionally create Instagram Boost campaigns through the Meta Marketing API.

2. Information we process

Depending on how you use the App, we may process:

  • Meta access tokens you paste or authorize, used only to call Meta APIs on your behalf.
  • Instagram Business Account data returned by Meta, such as profile details, follower counts, media captions, thumbnails, permalinks, and available insights.
  • Facebook Page and Ad Account metadata needed for discovery and Boost flows (for example Page ID, Ad Account ID, currency, timezone, status).
  • Boost configuration you submit, such as objective, audience targeting inputs, budget, and schedule, which are sent to Meta to create Campaign, Ad Set, Creative, and Ad objects.
  • Standard technical logs generated by our hosting provider (for example request timestamps and error messages) for reliability and debugging.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate with Meta and retrieve your Instagram analytics dashboard data.
  • To create or manage Boost / Marketing API objects when you explicitly confirm creation.
  • To display results in the App UI and show Meta error messages when a request fails.
  • To improve reliability and diagnose API or deployment issues.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your Meta data for unrelated advertising.

4. How data is stored

  • Access tokens are intended to be held in browser memory for the active session and forwarded to our API proxy only to call Meta. They are not designed to be permanently stored in our database.
  • Dashboard media, profile, and insights data are loaded for display in your session and are not intended to be retained as a long-term personal data archive by the App.
  • When you create a Boost, Meta stores the resulting Campaign / Ad Set / Creative / Ad objects in your Meta Ad Account under Meta’s own terms and policies.

5. Sharing with third parties

The App shares necessary credentials and parameters with Meta Platforms (Facebook / Instagram Graph API and Marketing API) to provide the requested features. Hosting infrastructure (for example Cloudflare Pages / Pages Functions or equivalent) may process request metadata as part of delivering the App. We do not share your Meta token or Instagram content with unrelated third-party advertisers.

6. Meta Platform compliance

Use of Instagram and Marketing API data complies with Meta’s Platform Terms, Developer Policies, and applicable Instagram / Marketing API terms. You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to access the Meta assets (Pages, Instagram accounts, Ad Accounts) you connect.

7. Data retention

Session data is cleared when you disconnect or close the browser session. Boost objects created in Meta remain in your Ad Account until you delete or archive them in Meta Ads Manager. Server logs, if any, are retained only as long as needed for security and debugging.

8. Security

We use HTTPS in production and limit Marketing API write actions behind an explicit user confirmation step. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please protect your access tokens and revoke them in Meta if you suspect misuse.

9. Your choices

  • Disconnect inside the App to clear the in-session token and dashboard data from the UI.
  • Revoke App access from your Meta / Facebook settings.
  • Delete Campaigns, Ad Sets, or Ads in Meta Ads Manager.
  • Stop using the App at any time.

10. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction) and is intended for business / creator account operators.

11. International processing

Depending on where our hosting and Meta’s infrastructure operate, your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Meta’s processing is governed by Meta’s privacy policy and terms.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the POC evolves. The “Last updated” date below will change when material revisions are published at this URL.

13. Contact

For privacy questions about this App, contact the operator of the Meta app break.the.scroll through the contact details listed in the Meta App Dashboard, or via the project repository maintainer for Meta Social Manager.
